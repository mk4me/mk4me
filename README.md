# Marek Kulbacki

CEO & Chief Scientist [@DIVEINAI](https://diveinai.com) · Rector's Plenipotentiary for Technology Development [@PJATK](https://pja.edu.pl) · IEEE Senior Member

Working at the intersection of multimodal AI, clinical decision support, and human–technology symbiosis. Three decades across hardware/embedded, motion analysis, computer vision, and applied ML — most of it on systems where being wrong has consequences (medical, defense, industrial).

---

## Current focus

- **Human-Technology Symbiosis (HTS)** — integrating human augmentation with AI, robotics, and cognitive systems
- **Clinical AI & digital twins** — pediatric rehabilitation, cerebral palsy outcome prediction, multimodal patient modelling (long-running collaboration with [MCN Zagórze](https://centrumzagorze.pl))
- **System-of-systems architecture** for healthcare and research infrastructure
- **LLM evaluation methodology** for non-English clinical and professional contexts
